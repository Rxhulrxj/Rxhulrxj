I bridge the gap between **business strategy** and **shipped code**.

I lead engineering at **Suffix E Solutions** as CTO, and I still review code, design systems and ship features myself. Over 5+ years I've gone from Software Engineer to Project Head to CTO, building full-stack products, LLM-based features and the architecture underneath them.

- 🧭 **Now:** CTO & Senior Software Engineer, Suffix E Solutions *(Jul 2025 – present, Thiruvananthapuram)*
- 🤖 **Focus:** generative AI, LLM integration, RAG, agentic workflows and MCP
- 🏗️ **Also:** system design and technical direction for teams shipping more than one product
- 🎓 **Teaching:** instructor at Scope India
- 💬 **Ask me anything:** there's an **Ask AI** assistant on [my portfolio](https://rahulrajn.in) that answers questions about my work
- 🎨 **Off the clock:** drawing and gaming
